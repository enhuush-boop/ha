Scan a suspicious link

DNS, TLS certificate, domain age, and threat-intel checks. With a VirusTotal key configured, the URL is submitted for a fresh scan.

Do not open it in your browser
Safety first. Never enter credentials into a link you do not trust. VirusTotal submissions may become visible to its community, so do not scan URLs containing secrets, personal data, or private tokens.

Source library & journalist reviews

Sources and reviews are saved in a local SQLite database. A saved site is not automatically trustworthy; a journalist must inspect it. Reviewer names are self-reported, and opinions are shown individually without an automatic majority verdict.
